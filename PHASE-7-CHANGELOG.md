# 4X4 COUKOO — Phase 7 Changelog

## Visual Polish & Dynamic Atmosphere

### New cinematic layer
- Added a small screen-space visual-effects layer over the existing 3D weather and day/night systems.
- The layer reacts to real in-game weather, time of day, vehicle speed, boost state, and electrical storms.
- Includes night grading, rain streaks, snow, dust, subtle storm flash, and speed motion accents.

### Player controls and performance
- Added Visual Effects options: Auto, On, and Off.
- Auto keeps the feature enabled for Medium and High, then disables the optional layer for Low to protect mobile performance.
- The setting is stored in the existing versioned save, including cross-tab sync.

### Quality assurance
- Added automated Phase 7 source and save-compatibility checks.
